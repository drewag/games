# Vocabulary

If you searched for a word in the last column, it isn't one we use. The word in the
first column is the one you want.

| Term | Means | Defined in | We don't say |
|---|---|---|---|
| Racer | The pet you picked and are driving | racers.md | player character, driver, hero |
| Rival | One of the four computer-driven pets | racers.md | opponent, AI, CPU, enemy, bot |
| Kart | The food-shaped vehicle a pet rides | racers.md | car, vehicle |
| Power | A pet's one special move, used with a full meter | racers.md | ability, item, special, super |
| Meter | The 3-segment bar that fills from snacks | play.md | power bar, charge, energy |
| Snack | Food floating over the track | play.md | pickup, item, coin, collectible |
| Spin out | A rival's kart twirls and slows after a hit | play.md | knocked out, stunned, crash |
| Catch-up | Rivals easing off or speeding up to keep it close | play.md | rubber band, rubberbanding |
| Pick screen | The title screen with the five cards | play.md | character select, menu |
