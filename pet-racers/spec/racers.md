# The racers

The eight pets come from the hand-drawn character cards. Every pet appears in every race: the one
you pick is your **racer**, and the others are **rivals** driven by the computer. When
racing friends, each **friend**'s racer takes a rival's place.

Names follow the cards. **Duffy** is the yellow pet and **Mango** is the white calico with a
brown and an orange ear. The older games name them differently (Snack Attack calls the
yellow pet Mango); this game goes by the cards.

| Pet | Kart | Favorite food | Color | Power |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Buddy | Taco | taco | black | **Gaming Knockout** |
| Duffy | Salmon Nigiri | sushi | green | **Speed Eating** |
| Choco | Ramen Bowl | ramen | purple | **Sports Ball** |
| Mango | Sushi Boat | sushi | orange | **Instant Cake** |
| Fluff Puff | Popcorn Bucket | popcorn | pink | **Brave Mode** |
| Zeus | Sushi Burger | sushi and burgers | yellow | **Math Defeater** |
| Plufulous | Burger Kart | burgers | pink | **Instant Hacking** |
| Scarlet Ivy | Maki Roll | sushi | blue | **Fashion Shoot** |

A pet's color comes from "Fav color" on their card. Zeus lists two favorite foods, so both
sushi and burgers fill two meter segments for Zeus. Their power's effects are drawn in it.

## Powers

Every power is one button press that uses a full **meter** (see [play](play.md#snacks-and-the-meter)).
Each one gives the pet its own way to race:

- **Gaming Knockout** (Buddy): a ring bursts out from the kart, and every rival within about
  two kart-lengths spins out. Buddy wants to be in the middle of the pack.
- **Speed Eating** (Duffy): a 3-second sugar rush at 1.4× speed. Duffy also gets a tiny
  boost from every snack eaten, so Duffy wants to grab everything.
- **Sports Ball** (Choco): Choco kicks a ball down the track. It bounces off the hedges
  and spins out the first rival it touches, then pops. Choco wants to be chasing someone.
- **Instant Cake** (Mango): a cake appears on the track behind the kart. The first rival
  to drive into it stops to eat it, which counts as spinning out. Cakes last 20 seconds.
  Mango wants to be in front.
- **Brave Mode** (Fluff Puff): for 4 seconds Fluff Puff can't spin out, drives on the grass
  at full speed, and bumps aside any rival it touches, spinning them out. Fluff Puff
  wants to take the short way across the grass.
- **Math Defeater** (Zeus): a big yellow plus sign flies down the track after the nearest
  kart ahead (if it's within about 25 kart-lengths) and spins it out. With nobody ahead it just flies
  on. Zeus wants to be right behind someone.
- **Instant Hacking** (Plufulous): Plufulous hacks the race and pops up two kart-lengths in
  front of the next kart ahead, in a burst of pink pixels. With nobody close ahead it jumps
  a little way on instead. It doesn't spin anyone out. Plufulous wants to be stuck just
  behind someone.
- **Fashion Shoot** (Scarlet Ivy): a camera flash goes off, and every kart beside or
  behind Scarlet Ivy within about five kart-lengths is dazzled and spins out ("Flash!").
  A friend who gets flashed sees their screen flash white. Scarlet Ivy wants to be just in
  front of a crowd.

Rivals don't use powers. They can only be bumped or spun out, which keeps races friendly
for younger players.

The powers come from the "Powers" line on each card. Several cards also list love,
kindness and friendship, which are shared by everyone and aren't powers here. Plufulous's
card also lists math defeating, but that one's Zeus's, so Plufulous gets hacking; Scarlet
Ivy's card lists hacking too, so Scarlet Ivy gets the glamorous fashion shoots.
