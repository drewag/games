# How it plays

Pet Racers is a kart racer: three laps of one track against four rivals. The hard parts
of the genre are taken away for a 9-year-old. The kart accelerates by itself, you can't
fall off or get stuck, and nobody is far behind for long.

## Picking a pet

The title screen shows the five card scans in a row. Tapping a card picks that pet: it
appears in its kart, slowly turning, with the kart's name and the power's name under it.
**Race!** starts. The game remembers the last pet picked.

## Controls

The game is played in landscape on an iPad. On-screen buttons sit under the thumbs:

- **◀ ▶**, bottom-left, held to steer
- **Power**, bottom-right, labeled with the pet's power name. It lights up and wobbles
  when the meter is full.

With neither steering button held, the kart eases toward the road ahead on its own. That
makes steering a correction, not a constant job. Arrow keys and space work on a computer.

## A race

Karts start on a staggered grid behind the start line, with your racer at the back. A
3-2-1-GO countdown starts the race. The top of the screen shows the lap (1/3) and your
place (1st–5th). After the third lap, a card shows your place and time and offers
**Race again!** or a different pet.

- **Grass** beyond the red and white curbs slows the kart to about half speed.
- **Hedges** line the track. Driving into them slides the kart back along the road and
  costs some speed. It never stops the kart or turns it around.
- **Karts bump** each other apart. Bumping never spins anyone out (Brave Mode aside).
- **Catch-up**: rivals far ahead of you ease off, and rivals far behind speed up, so
  the race stays close. Rivals differ a little in top speed, so the order varies.

## Snacks and the meter

Snacks float over the track in rows across it: tacos, sushi, ramen and popcorn, mixed.
Driving through one eats it, and it comes back a few seconds later. The **meter** in the
top corner has 3 segments. A snack fills one; your pet's favorite food fills two. A full
meter lights up the Power button, and using the power empties it.

Rivals eat snacks too, which takes them away from you, but they have no meter.

## Spinning out

A rival hit by a power **spins out**: their kart twirls, they slow to a crawl for about
1.5 seconds, and "Bonk!" pops up. Then they carry on. Nothing in the game spins out your
own racer, because rivals don't use powers.
